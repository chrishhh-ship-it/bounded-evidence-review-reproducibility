## 4. Benchmark metadata and remaining boundary

The main query file records four domains, language, category, intent,
hallucination-challenge, and difficulty fields. Contributor A and Contributor B
independently assigned the difficulty and category labels, and a third member
of the research team adjudicated disagreements. The annotation process
therefore used two independent annotators plus an adjudicator. A
reliability coefficient for these particular fields is not stated here until
it is computed directly from the corresponding preserved annotation files;
the adjudication record alone is not treated as a substitute for that
calculation.

The current archive also does not document a separate independently
constructed held-out query set. The manuscript therefore does not claim
held-out generalizability; it reports the query provenance and narrows the
external-validity claim accordingly.
